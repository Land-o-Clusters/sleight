# Flag A/Bs, 2026-10-19

Open screenshots and screenshot guidance become defaults in 1.3.1. Compact confirmations are removed.
Compact toolbars stay opt-in because textedit-replace-all was 16% slower.

## Method

All 55 invocations ran build `60fe1319820053db382b26bdaecbc1db83e0c895`, Haiku 5.5
(`claude-haiku-5-5`) at high effort, on ChatGPT engine `26.1013.40934`.
Order alternated over five rounds: off first in odd rounds, last in even rounds. Each invocation ran
one attempt per task, except preview-read, which ran two, giving ten per side. Office ran last
and shared its off attempts between compact-toolbars and skill-screenshot.

The held-out tasks were textedit-edit, preview-pdf and textedit-replace-save for open-shot;
textedit-edit, preview-pdf, finder-create-folder and textedit-insert-line for compact confirmations;
textedit-replace-all, textedit-insert-line, word-memo and word-brief for compact toolbars;
and word-memo and word-brief for screenshot guidance. Open-shot also ran preview-read, the
claim task sleight lost. No held-out Excel task existed, and excel-edit was a claim task sleight led.

The scorer was `bench/flag-ab.mjs` at that build. Reproduce all four tables with
`node bench/flag-ab.mjs score --legacy --manifest ~/Projects/sleight-wt/race-1017/flags-manifest.tsv`.
The scorer also selects legacy mode when a manifest includes the confirmations arm. Expected seconds equal mean attempt seconds
divided by the pass rate. Total time sums task expectations. Each task's standard error is the
square root of the sum of the two sample variances divided by their run counts; the total combines
task errors in quadrature. These error estimates use attempt times and do not estimate uncertainty
in the pass rate.

The preset verdict rule makes a flag default on only when its total gain exceeds one standard
error, no task loses pass rate, and no task slows by more than 10% beyond its own standard error.
A gain inside the noise, a lost pass or such a slowdown keeps it opt-in. No gain removes it.
Fewer than three runs per side on any task, mixed commits or a dirty tree give `no verdict`.
The measured-win rule in LAWS/ROADMAP makes winners defaults without a user-facing opt-out; the remaining
environment switches serve A/Bs only.

Open-shot falls from 65.8 to 60.0 s (5.8 s faster, SE 5.2 s), with no lost pass.
The default includes a screenshot with every successful `open_file` acquisition.
`SLEIGHT_OPEN_SHOT=0` is its A/B off switch.

Screenshot guidance falls from 196.2 to 162.0 s (34.2 s faster, SE 22.7 s). The off side lost
one word-memo attempt. The on side passed every attempt. The off side was the `60fe1319`
default users received, with the textless hint and its read handling already on. The on side added only the
measured sentence to the default skill. The default now includes that exact sentence. The lean skill stays at
its `60fe1319` text because this A/B never ran it. `SLEIGHT_SCREENSHOT_READS=0` removes the marked
sentence and disables the standalone compactor's screenshot reads. The relay keeps its original
textless-hint setting, including its compactor override, on both sides. `SLEIGHT_TEXTLESS_HINT=0`
still disables that hint and its open-file fallback. The new planner uses the screenshot switch
for the screenshot arm's off side. `SLEIGHT_SKILL=none` still omits the skill entirely.

Compact confirmations had no win (90.9 to 92.3 s), and textedit-edit slowed 14.4%. Its flag and
compaction code are removed. The full confirmation policy is unchanged. Compact toolbars stay
opt-in: textedit-replace-all went from 23.2 to 26.9 s (+16.2%, SE 2.4 s), despite the Office gain.

The tables retain the historical flag names used by build `60fe1319`. Raw numeric attempts,
including failures, are in [open-shot](2026-10-19-flag-ab-open-shot.json), [compact-confirmations](2026-10-19-flag-ab-compact-confirmations.json), [compact-toolbars](2026-10-19-flag-ab-compact-toolbars.json), [skill-screenshot](2026-10-19-flag-ab-skill-screenshot.json).
