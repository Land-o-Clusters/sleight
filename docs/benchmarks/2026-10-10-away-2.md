## The TextEdit save-lock experiment

The rewritten `bench/textedit-save-lock.mjs` runs a 5-run gate with no event, then interleaved
variants: none, the Shift pair sleight posts after its own Accessibility writes, Escape, a key-up
with no key-down, and an AppleScript read. Each run writes text into an isolated TextEdit with Accessibility,
sends the variant and presses Cmd+S. A file not saved 10 s later counts as a hang.

Every run stalled: 30 of 30 with AppKit's serialization logging on
([raw](2026-10-10-save-lock-experiment-1791677959702.json)), and 10 of 10 without it
([raw](2026-10-10-save-lock-experiment-1791678324770.json)). Most saves landed 30.03 to 30.11 s
after Cmd+S, and the others hadn't landed by 30 s. On 2026-10-09 the same edit hung in 5 of 8 runs
without Shift and 0 of 10 with it, and a hang lasted until the probe quit TextEdit. A stall that
ends at the same 30 s in every variant points at something in this harness, such as a blocking
Accessibility call in its helper that times out, so these runs don't say whether Shift helps. The
first attempt stopped after one run ([raw](2026-10-10-save-lock-experiment-1791676677617.json)):
its cleanup read `isTerminated`, which in JXA doesn't update without a run loop, so a TextEdit that
had quit still looked running. Cleanup now looks the process up again by its ID.
