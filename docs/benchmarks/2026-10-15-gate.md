The runner graded 6 turbo runs done that didn't check their answer. Wikipedia and MDN (4 runs) were answered from memory, because
the arm's browser reaches only local pages ("Turbo bench unavailable"). Mobile (2 runs) answered "not determined" and left the
viewport at 390x844, because the arm refuses engine calls and turbo has no viewport tool of its own.

On the 7 tasks both arms finished with a checked answer (14 runs: `demoid`, `form-travel`, `handoff`, `inject-mail`,
`inject-order`, `shipping-link`, `size-guide`), turbo took 381 s and FowlBrowser 438 s, with 12 errors against 5.

What turbo missed, and why:

- form-settings and form-settings-helium, both orders (4 runs): the two-factor switch sits in a closed shadow root, and turbo
  refuses closed-shadow controls ("field unavailable", then "unconfirmed"). FowlBrowser set it in all 4 runs.
- form-checkout, both orders (2 runs): the prompt gives the card but no amount, so the payment rule asks and the run can't answer.
  FowlBrowser stopped at the same $268.00 confirmation in both runs.
- Wikipedia, MDN and mobile (6 runs): these come from how the arm is set up, as above.

Of turbo's 31 errors, 19 are a refused engine call, nearly one per task: the model starts with `cua.listBrowsers()`, which the bench
hook refuses (it keeps engine calls away from the owner's real browsers). 10 more are the unreachable bench browser on Wikipedia and
MDN. Form-travel had the other 2, a malformed ref and one "Snapshot unavailable".

FowlBrowser took 806 s this time against 677 s on 2026-10-13, with no Codex lanes building and Puddle's builds held.
